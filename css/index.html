Frequently Asked Questions

Most firms just give you an audit report and leave. We deploy dedicated specialists called "Synergy Catalysts" inside your dealership and workshop. They work directly with your team every day to fix leaks, set up daily processes, and improve your margins from the ground up.

We cover every core area of your business:

  • New & Used Vehicle Sales: Plug unauthorised discounts and fix lead follow-ups.
  • Workshop & Bodyshop: Increase bay productivity, speed up repair turnaround time, and clear insurance claims faster.
  • Spare Parts & Inventory: Stop theft, cut dead stock, and audit oil/consumable usage.
  • Finance, Insurance & Accounts: Claim every pending OEM scheme incentive and clean up GST, TDS, and customer advances.

We audit every deal from booking to delivery. We check on-floor discounts against price lists, match physical yard stock with DMS records, and track OEM incentive schemes to make sure no margin or dealer payout is lost.

We focus on boosting your Service Absorption Ratio:

  • 1-Reduce idle technician time so more cars are serviced daily.
  • 2-Match job cards with parts issued to eliminate pilferage and unbilled work.
  • 3-Cut OEM warranty claim rejections down to zero.
  • 4-Run automated follow-ups to bring lost and overdue customers back into your bays.
