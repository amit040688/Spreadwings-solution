Frequently Asked Questions

Unlike conventional advisory firms that only deliver third-party observation reports, we operate under a hands-on "Co-Enabling Synergy" model. We deploy dedicated on-ground specialists ("Synergy Catalysts") directly inside your business locations to work alongside your department heads and staff. We don’t just identify problems—we actively implement standard operating procedures (SOPs), execute corrective actions, and track daily operational progress from within.

We provide 360-degree management consulting. While we have specialised, deep-rooted expertise in automotive dealership networks (Sales, Bodyshop, Workshop/Service, and Pre-Owned/True Value operations), our multi-disciplinary team also drives end-to-end transformation across:

  • Financial Due Diligence, Taxation (GST/TDS/RCM), & Accounting
  • Corporate Legal Advisory & Regulatory Compliance
  • Enterprise IT & Digital Transformation (AI/ML integration)
  • Business Development, Channel Marketing, & Loyalty Programs
  • Human Resource (HR) Solutions & Staff Skill Training

Our Synergy model pairs daily on-ground execution with high-level senior oversight:

  • Permanent On-Site Catalyst: A dedicated specialist deployed at your facility to monitor day-to-day operations and enforce process discipline.
  • Specialist & Industry Expert Visits: Periodic audits and reviews conducted by senior automotive industry veterans and Chartered Accountants (CAs).
  • Structured Reporting (RYG Format): Bi-monthly/monthly Red-Yellow-Green (RYG) dashboards tracking deviations, KPIs, and resolution timelines.
  • Leadership Strategy Reviews: Regular coordination meetings with General Managers and Dealer Principals for Month-on-Month (MOM) strategic planning.

We implement a rigorous Sales Synergy Framework that audits transactions from docket to delivery:

  • Daily cash discount monitoring to preserve dealer margins.
  • Validation of billing against official price lists, quotations, and RTO/insurance invoices.
  • Physical stock-to-DMS reconciliation to detect unbilled deliveries or mismanaged demo vehicles.
  • Timely reconciliation of OEM schemes, credit notes, and customer benefits to ensure full claim realisation.
  • In-house finance and insurance renewal tracking to maximise payout capture.

Our Workshop Synergy Programme enhances throughput and cuts waste by:

  • Tracking technician efficiency and bay productivity against job card allocations to reduce idle workshop time.
  • Validating spare part requisitions against physical replacements to eliminate pilferage and control dead stock.
  • Auditing warranty and free-service documentation to adhere strictly to OEM guidelines, driving down claim rejection rates.
  • Systematising service follow-up and appointment calling to increase customer retention and reduce turn-off ratios.
